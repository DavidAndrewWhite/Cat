## Page A  

### Panel 1 *(Full page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
**Peter Cat** hovers over a fenced-in area filled with warehouses. A swath of destruction leads to a gaping hole in one of the warehouses.  
#### Title  
> **Peter Cat** and the **Nihilistic Gorger** in... *JUST CAN'T GET ENOUGH.*  
#### **Peter Cat**  
> Aha!  
> I see the *Nihilistic Gorger* has been busy!  
### Panel 2 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
**Peter Cat** touches down and looks through the hole in the warehouse wall. The **Nihilistic Gorger** rips open another crate of delicious Twinkies™ snack cakes.  
#### **Peter Cat**  
> He simply *cannot* resist those delicious Twinkies™!  
> I wonder why...  
### Panel 3 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
**Peter Cat** uses his senses to detect a landscape of chemicals, depicted symbolically in the background.  
#### **Peter Cat**  
> I *see* now!  
> Partially hydrogenated vegetable oil!  
> BHT!  
> *Polysorbate-60!*  
### Panel 4 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
**The Magister** appears out of nowhere, and rushes to stand in front of **Peter Cat**, arms spread; this interrupts **Peter Cat's** concentration.  
#### **The Magister**  
> No!  
> The *world* isn't *ready* for the *secrets* of that *delicious cream filling!*  
### Panel 5 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **Nihilistic Gorger** has swallowed a huge mass of Twinkies -- possibly including the wrappers -- and looks pitifully into the readers' eyes.  
#### **Nihilistic Gorger**  
> No matter how much I consume--  
> --*nothing* fills the *emptiness inside!*  
#### Box *(lower right, resembling a ribbon)*  
> You'll get a big delight  
> in every bite  
> of Hostess™ Twinkies™ Cakes  

## Page B  

### Panel 1 *(Full page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
An abandoned temple to some forgotten god. On a pedestal sits a single Hostess™ Cupcake™ bathed in unnatural light. The **Mad Scholar** rushes to seize this treasure with a frenzied, ecstatic look on his face. **Jinx Bubastis** hides behind a stone altar, grinning with sinister intent.  
#### Title  
> **Jinx Bubastis** and the **Mad Scholar** in "WISH FULFILLMENT."  
#### **Mad Scholar**  
> At *last!*  
> I have *found* it *at last!*  
#### **Jinx Bubastis** *(thought balloon)*  
> *Hah!* The *Mad Scholar* is *blundering* right into my *trap!*  
### Panel 2 *(1/3rd page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The Cupcake grows to monstrous size, dwarfing the cowering **Mad Scholar**.  
#### **Mad Scholar**  
> What!?  
#### Box *(narration, between Panels 2 and 3)*  
> And before the *Mad Scholar's* eyes, the delicious Hostess™ Cupcake™ *seems* to grow to *titanic size!*  
### Panel 3 *(1/3rd page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **Mad Scholar** cowers in terror before the awesome sight.  
#### Box *(narration, between Panels 3 and 4)*  
> Soon he feels himself *falling* into the *gravitational field* of the planet-sized treat!  
### Panel 4 *(1/3rd page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **Mad Scholar** is a tiny figure, falling helplessly as he falls into a planet-sized Hostess™ Cupcake™.
#### **Mad Scholar** *(weakly, losing the last of his sanity)*  
> ...my mind is going, I can feel it...  
### Panel 5 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
**Jinx Bubastis'** hand snatches the Hostess™ Cupcake™ from the delirious **Mad Scholar's** nerveless grasp.  
#### **Jinx Bubastis**  
> Oh, sweetie, you've been *gone* a *while now...*  
> ...your *obsession* with delicious Hostess™ Cupcakes™ made your *mind easy to break!*  
> Now, let's *see...*  
### Panel 6 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
**Jinx Bubastis** has taken a bite of the Hostess™ Cupcake™ and chews thoughtfully.   
#### **Jinx Bubastis**  
> Meh, it's *OK* I guess...  
#### Box *(lower right, resembling a ribbon)*  
> You'll get a big delight  
> in every bite  
> of Hostess™ Cupcakes™
