## Issues \#1-\#4: *"Leave Home"*  

- **Issue \#1:** There's something in the sky, a huge structure made of RF energy, and something in the middle of it is sending a crude distress signal. **Jinx Bubastis** breaks her old love **Peter Cat** out of containment to investigate. [*PDF*](pdf/Issue%201.pdf) [*Source*](Issue%201.md)  
- **Issue \#2:** **Camillo** and **Penny Skragg** discover a universe their species left an indelible mark upon, while the past starts catching up with **Peter Cat** and **Jinx Bubastis**. [*PDF*](pdf/Issue%202.pdf) [*Source*](Issue%202.md)  
- **Issue \#3:** **Honor Guard** and **M-Seven** have a confrontation in Earth orbit, and then things get worse. Now humanity is asking, "Who had the bright idea of giving pro wrestlers nuclear weapons?" [*PDF*](pdf/Issue%203.pdf) [*Source*](Issue%203.md)  
- **Issue \#4:** **Peter Cat**, **Jinx Bubastis**, and **Camillo** and **Penny Skragg** have cut a deal with human authorities. No one is innocent, and no one is escaping unscathed. [*PDF*](pdf/Issue%204.pdf) [*Source*](Issue%204.md)  

## Issue \#5: *"A Mission to Carcosa"* 

- **The King in Yellow** knows many things, but how much of *you* is left after you learn them? Come visit *Carcosa*, a place where dreams are public space and text is ephemeral, and see a very human kind of madness.  [*PDF*](pdf/Issue%205.pdf) [*Source*](Issue%205.md)
