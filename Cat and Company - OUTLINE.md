# **"Cat & Company" – Outline for a Comics Series**  
[REFERENCE ONLY - NOT STORY] 
---

## **I. Setting & Time Period**  
- **Location**:  
  - **Earth**: Remote Catskill Mountains (Camillo’s home), corporate-controlled space stations, and alien colonies.  
  - **Carcosa**: A secretive archipelago off Portugal, ruled by **The King in Yellow** (human survivor).  
  - **Space**: Corporate-controlled star cluster 400 light-years from Earth; remnants of the **Reyll Theocracy** (Fe Arran).  

- **Time Period**:  
  - **Contemporary 21st century Earth**, with flashbacks to prehistoric civilizations.  

- **Infrastructure**:  
  - Earth’s infrastructure is **primitive** (e.g., no fusion reactors), while **Fe Arran colonies** feature **surreal, hyper-efficient architecture**.  
  - **Reyll settlements** blend **ancient mysticism with advanced science** (e.g., psychic networks).  
  - **Corporate media** exploits Earth’s "primitive" state as a **sacred mystery**.  

- **Atmosphere**:  
  - **Absurdist satire** (e.g., corporate "worked" fights as entertainment) meets **existential dread** (humans as ancient "failed investments").  
  - **Core joke**: Fe Arrans (who are *younger* than humans) expect humans to be **illuminated overlords and precursors**—but we’re **technologically and socially backward**.

## **II. Alien Civilizations & Factions**  

### **Fe Arrans**
- **Humanoid species**: Bear/cat hybrids, superficially feline.  
- **Very young**: The modern form of their species is under 100,000 years old. Their species has a high level of genetic diversity, with significant differences in bone structure among individuals.  
- **Home systems**: Native to a star cluster 400 light-years from Earth, hundreds of potentially habitable systems within a few dozen light-years of each other.  
- **Civil war**: **The Corporation** and **the Reyll Theocracy** fought a brutal civil war. The Reyll were defeated, and exist as scattered remnants and cultural artifacts -- see **M-Seven** under "Minor Characters".  

#### **1. The Corporation**  
- **Composition**: **Fe Arran technocratic empire** (cat/bear-like aliens), predating humanity.  
- **Ideology**: **Survivalism, efficiency, domination**.  
  - **Taboo**: Earth is **sacred ground** (hidden source of "ancient human wisdom").  
- **Clones**: **cloned Fe Arrans** (e.g., Peter) are **mass-produced tools**, not people —- stripped of autonomy for labor/warfare.  
- **Cultural Control**:  
  - **Paranormal "worked" fights**: Scripted battles between **"face" teams** (Honor Guard) and **"heel" teams** (M-Seven).  
  - **Example**: Peter (ex-Honor Guard) vs. Jinx (ex-M-Seven) in a corporate spectacle.  

#### **2. The Reyll Theocracy**  
- **Composition**: **Ancient Fe Arran faction**, valuing **truth, unity, and intellectual freedom**.  
- **Beliefs**:  
  - **Rejects gods**: seeks **truth of existence**.  
  - **Cloning is immoral**: Clones lack moral agency, creating living beings without moral agency is an abomination, as it undermines the value of life and growth.  Contrasted with attitudes towards artificial intelligence -- creating artificial intelligences is seen as a valuable transcendence of mortality.  
  - **Artifical intelligence**: Views the construction of AI as a transcendent exercise in art and engineering, makes extensive use of autonomous robots that are treated as children.  
  - **Values diversity**: Attempted to preserve the cultural artifacts and languages of conquered peoples **within their existing moral and linguistic framework**.  
  - **Practical moralists**: Reject gods and discourage research into the Necrosphere, concerned with recognizing good and evil in everyday life.  
- **History**:  
  - **Imperial Phase**: Integrated cultures into Fe Arran society.  
  - **Fall**: Became a **totalitarian military dictatorship** during war with the Corporation.  
- **Suppression of Paranormals**:  
  - **Strong paranormals** labeled **mental illness**; **Rrmu Nrwwa** promoted **compassion over might**.  

### **3. The Space Bankers**  
- **Overview**:  
  - **Ancient, inhuman entities** that view humans as **“failed investments”**.  
  - Control a **cosmic economy of souls**, using **death and afterlife as currency**.  

- **Economy of Souls**:  
  - **Necrosphere**: A **secondary ecosystem** where **Demons** act as **miners**, extracting **memories and attachments** from the dead.  
  - **Lake of Fire**: Inferno where mortal contradictions burn out; beneath lies the **World of Ash** (a surreal, empty echo of the waking world).  
  - **Fungibility**: Souls stripped of ego become **transferable**; used as **gas fees** for cosmic blockchains or reborn as new individuals.  

- **Relationship with Demons**:  
  - **Analogous to humans and yeast**: Demons spontaneously attach to complex ecosystems.  
  - **Hostile to mortals**: Demons view human morality as **self-serving delusion**.

## **IV. Main Characters**  

### **1. Peter Cat**  
- **Species**: **Fe Arran** (Cloned Series 350 Engineer).  
- **Role**: **Walking weapon** (energy/matter manipulation); **ex-Honor Guard** ("face" team).  
- **Abilities**: Energy and matter manipulation coming from an ability to subvert entropy, like the **Maxwell's demon** though experiment.  
- **Conflict**: PTSD from past as weaponized paranormal. Aspires to be a gentle, amiable, and benevolent superhero, but cannot escape his talent for cold, clinical violence.  
- **Personality**: Gentle, goofy humor; **unassuming despite terrifying power**.  Can change in an instant into a walking weapon of mass destruction.  

### **2. Jinx Bubastis**  
- **Species**: **Fe Arran** (demon-fused).  
- **Role**: **Former M-Seven member** ("heel" team); now seeks **meaning in chaos**.  
- **Abilities**: Demonic strength, teleportation, mind-reading, magic.  
- **Conflict**: Betrayed M-Seven after realizing a terrorist attack would kill millions. More generally, rejects nihilism in favor of meaning, frustrated with being a simple villian.  
- **Dynamic**: **Calvin & Hobbes-esque** with Peter (abrasive vs. gentle).  

### **3. Camillo Skragg**  
- **Species**: **Human** (ex-engineer).  
- **Role**: **Mental patient** building alien power armor to reclaim identity.  
- **Background**: Mind-damaged by **secret human research into Corporate tech**, subjected by human authorities to a crude form of mind-wipe along the lines of MKULTRA.  
- **Personality**: Introverted, obsessive, **loves music** (like Penny).  

### **4. Penny Skragg**  
- **Species**: **Human** (Camillo’s younger cousin).  
- **Role**: **Slacker** with reality-warping powers (triggered by "pretending" to be a martial artist).  
- **Personality**: **Free spirit**, loves bad movies, **absurdist humor**.

## **V. Antagonists**  

### **1. The Magister**  
- **Species**: **human**, but immortal and possesing eldritch powers. Comparable to (and may be the same person as) the **Comte de Saint Germain**.  
- **Role**: **Human scam artist** manipulating **Earth, the Corporation, and the Space Bankers**.  
- **Goal**: Merge Earth with Corporation to build **interstellar empire**.  
- **Method**: Uses **geomantic powers** and **Akashic Records** (Lemurian psychic network).  
- **Conflict**: Views **Cat & Company** as tools to **eliminate rivals** (e.g., direct attack on King in Yellow).  
- **Personality**: A glib fast-talker with dark charisma.  

### **2. The King in Yellow**  
- **Species**: **Atlantean post-human**, his immortal mind has experienced life in many different bodies, including a "flash mind" inhabiting the photosphere of Arcturus. He carefully guards what remains of his original body as an anchor to reality.  
- **Role**: **Human survivor** of Space Bankers’ extermination; ruler of **Carcosa**.  
- **Abilities**: **Possession** via **Yellow Sign** (marks humans, posession causes permanent and irreversible changes to the minds of victims).  
- **Conflict**: Hesitates to act against Magister (fears extinction event).  
- **Personality**: **Grumpy, sardonic, ancient** (has "seen everything").  

### **3. Tiamat**
- **Species**: **Ancient entity**, billions of years old, vaguely resembling a fractal serpent or dragon.  Her body everts into normal space-time from a finite pocket of dimensions orthogonal to normal space-time, comparable to the shell of a snail.  
- **Role**: Former owner of Earth, **creator goddess of the dinosaurs**. The collapse of the value of dinosaur souls led to her going deep into debt with the **Space Bankers**. Extinction of the dinosaurs caused by her attempt to liquidate assets to cover her debt.  
- **Abilities**: Reality manipulation to produce storms, extract souls, and live forever. Needs to evert into space-time to alter our reality. Technically mortal, as will be established in the story.  
- **Conflict**: Has threated to exterminate humanity on several occasions, banished to an Atlantean prison shunt (a Klein bottle like geometry of orthogonal dimensions) anchored in space-time near Bermuda by Atlantean wizards shortly before the fall of Atlantis. Owes the **Space Bankers** principal worth several billion fungible human souls.  
- **Personality**: Incomprehensible, malicious, wishes to settle her debt by any means necessary.

## **VI: Minor characters**

### **1. Corporate super-teams**
- **Honor Guard**:
  - **Role**: "face" team, in kayfabe **space police**.
  - **Current roster**:
    - **Victor Bravo**: a young male Fe Arran super-soldier equipped with the Corporation's latest and greatest military gear. Recently installed as team leader.  
	- **Jolt**: an older female Fe Arran. Her body produces a very strong electrical charge which is channeled by a containment bodysuit. Veteran member of the team, former team leader.  
	- **Thresher**: a male Fe Arran cyborg with a full-body prosthesis optimized for zero gravity melee combat and boarding actions. Can shoot tethers, arms feature retractable chainsaws. Wears a face mask that somewhat resembles a hockey goalie's face mask.  
	- **The Eye**: a young, perky female Fe Arran. Born without eyes, her eyesockets are covered with mirrored lenses. She can see thanks to a magical amulet fused to the top of her sternum that gives her 360° vision and limited precognition. Acrobatic, fights with flashbang grenades and diamond-bladed laser knives. Her precognitive powers result in her often stating the blindingly obvious.  
  - **Former members**: include Peter Cat, working under the codename **Arsenal**.  
  
- **M-Seven**:
 - **Role**: "heel" team of kayfabe **terrorists**, a propaganda caricature of the late-stage Reyll theocracy.  
 - **Current roster**:
   - **Demonslayer**: a Reyll witch/priestess, exiled for the Reyll Theocracy for sadism and anti-social tendencies. Long time leader of M-Seven, which she runs like an abusive cult. A cruel caricature of Reyll beliefs and practices, exploited for Corporate propaganda. **Number One** of M-Seven.  
   - **Bearcat**: a Fe Arran analogue of a gorilla, uplifted by Demonslayer to a high level of intelligence. Slavishly loyal to Demonslayer. **Number Two** of M-Seven.  
   - **Killotron**: an advanced Reyll android resembling a mechanical Fe Arran. A glowing crystal on his chest allegedly contains "the soul of a poet". Also slavishly loyal to Demonslayer. **Number Three* of M-Seven.
   - **Vandal**: a young male Fe Arran who received a similar super-solider treatment as **Victor Bravo**, but deserted from the Corporate military. Only mentioned, left the tream with **The Freeze** due to Demonslayer's toxic leadership style. Formerly, **Number Four** of M-Seven.  
   - **The Freeze**: a young female Fe Arran with cryonic powers. Only mentioned, left the team with **Vandal**. Former **Number Five** of M-Seven.  
   - **Champion**: a large, muscular male Fe Arran. His left eye has been ruined, and he wears an eypatch. Flying brick with a nuclear fission metabolism, used to be a well-regarded superhero on a colony that failed to free itself from Corporate control (**Magrige**). He was hoping playing an evil Superman would be more fun than this. Despite suffering from severe burn-out, still has enough values to find Demonslayer loathsome, and enough power to stand up to her. **Number Six** of M-Seven.
   - **Number Seven**: does not exist. There is no Number Seven. Do **not** ask Demonslayer about it.  
 - **Former members**: Jinx was briefly associated with M-Seven under the codename "Arcana". Was **Number Six**, resigned when Demonslayer exploited a vague script to plan a "shoot" terrorist attack that would have killed millions.  

### **2. Human Paranormals**
- **The Ready Men**
 - **Role**: superpowered operatives working directly for **the Magister**.  
 - **Current roster**:
   - **Peregrine**: an older human male, whose studies of alchemy have taught him how to make a toxic "medicine" that lets him command spirits of fire and air.  
   - **Beowulf**: a large and powerful human male, incredibly strong and tough. Has a magic word to transform into an alter ego, but has forgotten it.  
   - **Sleep**: a female human telepath and dream-walker, can easily put crowds of people to sleep and cause short-term amnesia.
   - **The Maker**: a human male who can animate inanimate objects. Cowardly, shies away from conflict, preferring to let his constructs fight for him.  
   - **Image**: apparently, a woman of indeterminate age. Insubstantial, can become invisible, and teleport. Carries a pair of nickel-plated Colt 1911 pistols. A ghost, has been dead for years. With some help from Sleep, can enter the Necrosphere.  
 - **Notes**: this is about the best the Magister can manage using Earth's diminished population of paranormals. "We have the X-Men at home."

## **VII. Lost Civilizations & Legacies**  
- **Lemurians**:  
  - **Human precursors** (75,000 years ago); created **psychic internet** (now used by Magister).  
  - **Integrated early hominids** into modern humans.  
- **Atlanteans**:  
  - **Human survivors** (9600 BCE); remnants in **Carcosa** (advanced medieval tech).  
  - **Connection**: **Forerunners to Fascism**, sent more primitive humans to off-world colonies.  
  - **Black Oil**: A psycho-reactive substance that formed the shunts the Atlanteans used to travel between worlds. Now a dangerous, toxic substance that responds unpredictably to the presence of humans.  

---

## **VIII. Human History & Anomalies**  
- **Humans Are Older**: **Millions of years** of existence; **Fe Arrans expect us to be advanced precursors** but we’re **technologically primitive**.  
- **Space Bankers’ Target**: Early civilizations **destroyed as pests, like rats in an abandoned house** (hidden in myths).  
- **Religion Paradox**: Human religions assume **personal gods** → **pathological certainty**.

## **X. Supplemental Material**  

### **A. Economy of Souls (Cosmic Cryptocurrency Parody)**  
- **Necrosphere**: Demons mine **memories/attachments** from dead (not Fe Arrans).  
- **Lake of Fire**: Purges mortal contradictions; beneath it lies the **World of Ash**, echoes of the waking world.  
- **Fungibility**: Souls stripped of ego = **gas fees** for cosmic blockchains.  

### **B. Paranormal Teams (Fe Arran Culture)**  
| **Team Type** | **Example**     | **Role**                          | **Alignment**      |
|---------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------|--------------------|
| **Face**      | Honor Guard     | Corporate police (scripted fights) | "Good" (Corporation) |
| **Heel**      | M-Seven         | Terrorist group (Reyll renegades)  | "Villain" (Corporation) |

- **Key Detail**: **M-Seven** was a **Reyll mage’s "heel" team** (not Corporation’s), led by "Demonslayer."

### **C. The King in Yellow & Carcosa**  
- **King**: **Human survivor** (not Fe Arran); **rules Carcosa** (Atlantean remnants).  
- **Carcosa**: **Hidden node** in cosmic conflict; **Yellow Sign** marks *humans* for possession.

## **XI. Themes & Philosophical Inquiry**  
- **Identity**:  
  - **Fe Arran clones** (Peter) vs. **human souls** (Camillo).  
  - **Jinx**: Fusion of **mortal/demon** (hybrid Fe Arran/Demon).  
- **Power**:  
  - **Corporation**: Control via cloning.  
  - **Reyll**: Truth-seeking (now fractured).  
  - **Space Bankers**: Souls as currency.  
- **Satire**:  
  - **Corporate "worked" fights** (Honor Guard vs. M-Seven).  
  - **Fe Arrans misjudging humans** (comedy of errors).  
- **Ethics**: **Immortality** (Space Bankers) vs. **mortality** (human struggle).
