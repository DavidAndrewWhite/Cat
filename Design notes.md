## Jinx Bubastis

Earlier iterations of Jinx were a lot shorter and a lot scrawnier. I kind of like your interpretation, though.

Jinx was the colorful member of the team. Peter and Penny were mostly dressed in black, Camillo was matte metallic, and she was the burst of color with combat boots and a black leather jacket. Brightly colored tights, a sarong-like skirt held up with a broad, ornate metal belt with a large scabbard holding a ritual dagger, a brightly colored t-shirt or tank top that might change color and design more or less at random.

Honestly, I don't know how well this kind of design has aged. There's a lot to be said for making her more substantial and giving her more physical presence. I still like the idea of her being colorful, though.

# Technology notes

## Weapons

- **Fe Arran Laser Weapons** are not like real-world laser systems like THEL or HELLADS. In visual terms, they are closer in presentation to the blasters from "Star Wars" -- they shoot what appear to be very fast-moving "bolts" from something that would look like a telephoto camera lens. The optic produces a moving focal point (the bolt) that provides data to the adaptive optics (the telephoto lens) on how to send a damaging signal to the final target at about 1.5 kW that will attack chemical bonds. The result is a sparky explosion. In theory, there is a "stun" setting, which is closer in effect to "get hit by a heavyweight boxer" that dumps energy into the atmosphere immediately in front of the target. These have some features useful for police work, like not shooting through starship or space station outer hulls or pressure bulkheads. 
- **Military Weapons** used by troopers in powered armor tend to be GPMG or SAW-sized "pulse cannon" that shoot solitons (self-contained waves) made of neutrinos. This is a platform that was gradually miniaturized from starship and gunpod (flying tank, basically) patterns into something that could be powered by portable fusors and wielded by troops with some assistance from powered armor. Shields are only marginally effective against these solitons, and armor designs need to be made of relatively low-density materials and use bulky layers of neutrino waveguides to diffuse the bolts. This will be discussed a little more in the section on armor. The bolts penetrate deeply through most matter and cause a significant number of nuclei to undergo reverse beta decay, effectively voting them off the "island of stability". The probability of this reaction increases with the atomic number of the nucleus. The effects of one of these bolts on the high-density materials used by humans for protection are horrific -- striking depleted uranium with one of these bolts will produce a combination of rapid, explosive heating, a deadly pulse of neutrons, and X-rays from fission products producing Bremsstrahlung, like a shaped charge with an extra flash of blue light.  
- **Specialist Weapons** useful against powered armor and light gunpods include shaped charges and high-speed penetrators, much like IRL anti-armor weapons. Relativistic neutron beams and small enhanced radiation fusion bombs play a role similar to IRL napalm and white phosphorous in dealing with fortifications.

## Armor

- **Powered Armor** designs currently in use by Fe Arrans are in an awkward place. Shield emitters explode if overloaded, so need to be installed in blow-out compartments, like ammunition in IRL armored fighting vehicles, and are only marginally effective against pulse cannon bolts. Armor panels tend to be bulky but very light-weight; an outer layer of "adamantine boron nitride" (a very hard but very brittle material) bonded to shock-absorbing polymers around largely hollow tuned waveguides that will disperse at least some neutrinos. Even using a "walking tank" powered armor platform doesn't offer that much protection against enemy fire. The main utility of the most common powered armor patterns is making it easy to carry a portable fusor, and using it to power heavy weapons, mobility-enhancing limited flight systems, and advanced sensors and communications gear. Most of these platforms can be fully sealed, making them useful for vacuum or toxic environments, and the neutron-absorbing properties of the boron nitride layer offer useful protection against some forms of radiation. Modern Earth anti-armor weapons will work very well against current Fe Arran designs. Another issue is fitting Fe Arran armor. Fe Arrans have a larger degree of physiological variation than humans, which makes engineering powered armor for Fe Arrans complex. While these complex designs can acommodate a human operator, the end result is far from comfortable and is of very limited practical use.
