## Page 1  

### Panel 1 *(Full page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **Black Sabbath** on landing approach over Main St in Phoenecia, NY. The area is not heavily populated, but the center of town has a number of small markets and bar and grills. 
#### **Camillo Skragg** *(from inside crew hull)*  
> All right, just a bit further out of the middle of town.  
> There's a spot *behind* the house, see if you can *set down there.*  
### Panel 2 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **Black Sabbath** has landed in the backyard of **Camillo Skragg's** house. **Camillo Skragg** and **Penny Skragg** lead **Jinx Bubastsis** and **Peter Cat** to the back door of the house.
#### **Penny Skragg**  
> It looks like there wasn't very much damage from when the troops pulled out.  
#### **Peter Cat**  
> That's good.  
### Panel 3 *(Half page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The kitchen and pantry of **Camillo Skragg's** house. **Peter Cat** and **Jinx Bubastis** look around with some curiosity. **Camillo Skragg** and **Penny Skragg** follow close behind.  
#### **Camillo Skragg**  
> On the *top floor* we have some *attic space.*  
> I was thinking you guys could set up there.  
### Panel 4 *(Full page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
As would be expected from attic space, there is a staircase and railing leading down roughly in the middle of the room. One eave has turned into a small but useful bathroom; otherwise, the space has been left open. Several round windows let in light from the other eaves. A double bed was moved up here into another eave a while ago -- neither Camillo nor Penny needed it -- and there are a handful of surplus wardrobes and dressers. **Peter Cat** and **Jinx Bubastis** look around.  
#### **Peter Cat**  
> Looks nice.  
#### **Jinx Bubastis**  
> Yeah.

## Page 3

### Panel 1 *(Full page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
A **young man** and a **young woman** seem to simply appear at one end of the table. **Peter Cat** and the others sit up and look at the strangers; the **Waitress** is frozen with surprise.  
#### **young man**  
> It is good we have found you.  
#### **young woman**  
> We bear an important message.  
#### **Peter Cat**  
> We're *listening.*  
### Panel 2 *(1/3rd page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **young man** kneels before the **young woman**, who places a gauzy pale yellow hood or veil over his head.  
#### **young woman**  *(to the **young man**)*  
> Are you ready?  
### Panel 3 *(1/3rd page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **young man** bows his head deeply, or droops. The shape of his skull seems to have changed; the forehead is large, round and protrudes outwards. The **Waitress** looks on in horror.  
#### **Waitress**  
> Is that some kind of mask?  
### Panel 4 *(1/3rd page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
The **young man** no longer looks like a young man -- he has become **The King in Yellow**. As he lifts his head and gets to his feet, he has the hunched posture of an ancient old man. Along with his over-sized forehead is a large, straight nose. He grasps the edge of the table with an ancient, frail hand.  
#### **The King in Yellow**  
> I wear no mask.  
### Panel 5 *(Full page width, 1/3rd page height)*:  
**The King in Yellow** stands next to the **young woman**, who appears to be in a trance. He stares down **Peter Cat**, **Jinx Bubastis**, **Camillo Skragg**, and **Penny Skragg**, while the **Waitress** stands there with a deer-in-the-headlights expression.  
#### **The King in Yellow**  
> Fear not.  
> I bear an *invitation.*  
> You must *come to Carcosa.*  
> You will be my guests.
